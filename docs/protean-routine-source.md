# The Protean Routine — source material (extracted from `reference_mat/Printable-Routines.pdf`)

Extracted 2026-08-01 with pdf-parse. This is the user's existing program and the seed for the app's
default plan. Format of each slot: `SETS x REPS-TIER-1/TIER-2/TIER-3` followed by a **progression
chain written hardest-first**, variants separated by `>`. The athlete picks the hardest variant they
can perform for the tier's rep count; rep tiers correspond to chain position (harder variant = fewer
reps).

## The Warm-Up

- 5 Minute Body Scan Meditation
- Knee Circles — 10 each direction
- Arm Windmills, opposite direction — 10 each way
- Alternating Toe Touches — 5 each side
- High Kicks — swing each leg straight up 5x, then sideways 5x
- Anterior Reach / CLA Reach — 5 each side
- Posterior Reach — 5 each side
- Bird Dog — 5 each side
- Deep Squats — 5 reps
- Balance Board (optional) — 2 minutes
- Reaction Ball-Wall OR Juggling OR Skipping — 2 minutes
- Skater Hops — 10 each side
- Overcoming Isometric — 4 x 7 seconds at 3 joint angles

## Notes for the app

- Week structure implied: Legs / Pull / Push / Full Body as training days + daily Warm-Up and
  Mobility as bookends. No prescribed day ordering or rest days in the PDF.
- Progression model: **chain + rep-tier**. Tiers are keyed to chain difficulty, not per-variant
  double progression. The app should generalize this: each slot = ordered variant list, each variant
  has a target rep band; advancing = moving toward the harder end of the chain.
- Distinctive methods to preserve: overcoming isometrics (4x7s at 3 angles), quasi-isometric
  (very-slow) finisher holds, loaded carries, crawling patterns, boxing conditioning, meditation
  bookends.
- The second sample (`training_progress_hex.jpg`, by u/KNightNox) is the visual model for the stunt
  tracker: 9 color-coded movement-family sectors of branching progression nodes radiating from a
  central muscle figure (push-up family, dips/planche/handstand, pull-up/levers/muscle-up, back
  chain, posterior legs, squat family, balance/acrobatics, running/swimming/martial arts, core).
